# SIYAJ: brand launch checks

SIYAJ is the working brand for technical AI agent assurance, synthetic-data security testing, and insurer-facing technical evidence intelligence in the UAE and Saudi Arabia.

## Brand status
The name SIYAJ / سياج is already used in other sectors, including an established Saudi group and a Saudi app. This kit does not establish legal clearance or ownership of a trademark.

Before public commercial use, instruct a UAE/KSA trademark lawyer to search Latin and Arabic variations (SIYAJ, SIAJ, SIYAG, سياج) in software, technology, cybersecurity, insurance-related and consulting classes. Check national company-name registers, trade marks, domains and social handles separately.

## Public product claims
AI assurance assessments and technical evidence only. Do not advertise insurance quotes, policy issuance, insurance brokerage, premium pricing or underwriting services without appropriate licences and partners. Demo-agent findings are scripted demonstrations, never customer proof.

## Go live
1. Complete any employer/conflict approvals.
2. Clear brand and entity registrations.
3. Review privacy policy and lead collection.
4. Upload `website/index.html` to GitHub Pages.
5. Run the local product from `product/SIYAJ_Assurance_Platform/start_mac.command`.
6. Use `strategy/` files for prospective partners and planning.
