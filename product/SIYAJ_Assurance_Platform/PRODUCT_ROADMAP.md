# From local MVP to enterprise insurance-risk platform

## Stage A — Now: controlled founder demonstrations

- Synthetic test cases, agent evidence, report downloads, technical insurance dossiers, explicit risk model and disclaimers.
- Deploy localhost only; demo to prospects using fictional records. Collect questionnaire feedback from cyber/financial-lines underwriters.
- Verify any live staging assessment and have an analyst sign off findings before sharing.

## Stage B — Paid design partners (4–8 weeks of focused engineering, estimate only)

- Implement isolated, disposable agent execution with deny-by-default egress and simulated tool side effects.
- Independent pentest, dependency lock / SBOM, encrypted storage and backup restoration tests.
- Dedicated staging tenant / environment per client; clear data deletion and consent.
- Verification artifacts for every self-attested control; secure document upload and access controls.
- Trial insurer question sets and structured evidence requirements validated by actual carriers.

## Stage C — Multi-organization SaaS

- Full OIDC/SSO, MFA, RBAC, tenant separation, audit sealing, signed reports and signed test-scope authorization.
- Secure cloud architecture, jurisdiction-approved region and subcontractor terms, usage metering / billing.
- Agent connectors under test with telemetry, replay and structured tools; continuous red teaming.
- Partner portal with explicit limited-time, per-dossier access and independent reviewer permissions.

## Stage D — Any regulated insurance proposition

- Obtain written UAE/KSA activity classification and counsel advice; partner with appropriately licensed entities.
- Insurer-validated actuarial / underwriting governance, transparent validation, audit and fairness controls.
- **Do not** implement quotes, commissions, rate indications, policy binding or sales until legally authorized.

**MVP go/no-go:** 3 confirmed agent-testing design partners, 1 underwriting evidence design partner, and 1 paid pilot agreement where legal/contracting gates are met. These are targets, not forecasts.
