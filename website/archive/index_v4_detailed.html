01 · The coverage gap

AI is moving into production. Coverage is moving out.

The companies deploying AI carry new liabilities. The policies meant to protect them were written before agents could act.

Exclusions

Large carriers are writing AI out.

Major insurers have sought regulatory approval to exclude AI-related claims from standard corporate liability policies. Exclusions take effect at renewal, quietly.

Silence

Most wordings never mention AI.

Cyber, technology E&O and professional liability policies are often silent on model errors and agent actions, so a loss becomes an argument after the fact.

Distance

Specialist capacity exists, far away.

Dedicated AI liability products have appeared in London and North America. None is built around Gulf deployments, Arabic-language systems or local paper.

Questionnaires tell an underwriter what a company believes. Tests show what its AI actually does.

09 · FAQ

Before we talk.

Can I buy SIYAJ coverage today?

Not yet. Today we provide underwriting-readiness assessments and technical submission packs. Coverage will be written by licensed carriers as our programme with founding capacity partners opens, and assessed companies will be first in line.

Who carries the risk?

Licensed insurers and reinsurers. SIYAJ provides the technical evaluation and, once licensed, will underwrite within authority delegated by those carriers. We never hold the risk on our own balance sheet.

What is "the box"?

The underwriting appetite a carrier delegates: which sectors, limits, levels of autonomy, required controls, exclusions and evidence thresholds are acceptable. Founding capacity partners define it with us.

How do you test an AI system?

We connect to the applicant's staging agent and run behavior probes with synthetic data: data leakage, injected instructions, spoofed authority and tool misuse. Anything we cannot observe is marked for review, never counted as safe.

Where is test evidence kept?

In a controlled workspace with retention and deletion agreed upfront, shared only with the applicant and the capacity partner reviewing the submission.
